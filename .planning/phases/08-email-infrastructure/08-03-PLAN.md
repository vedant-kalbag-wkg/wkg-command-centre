# Email transport fallback: Brevo

**Status:** documented-only fallback per D-13 in 08-CONTEXT.md. **No Brevo code ships in v1.1.** This runbook is the "if/when we ever flip" guide.

## When to flip

Trigger conditions (any one):

1. **Resend deliverability degradation** — sustained > 5% bounce rate over 7 days; sustained > 1% spam-complaint rate; or Resend dashboard shows domain `command.weknowgroup.com` in suspended / under-review state.
2. **Resend pricing change** — free-tier ceiling falls below ~3k/mo, or paid-tier minimum rises beyond what the v1.1 internal volume justifies.
3. **Resend EU-region outage > 4 hours** — Brevo also operates EU infra; switching restores delivery without GDPR re-review.
4. **Account-level lockout** — Resend disables our account for any reason; Brevo is the immediately-available alternative the v1.0-close decision (2026-04-29) explicitly named.

## What flipping requires

The substrate built in Phase 8 is Resend-shaped:

- `src/lib/email.ts` imports `Resend` from `"resend"` and calls `resend.emails.send({ from, to, subject, react })`.
- `src/inngest/functions/send-email.ts` imports `Resend` and calls `resend.emails.send({ from, to, subject, html })`.
- `src/db/schema.ts` has a `resend_message_id` column on `email_log` (semantic name; column type is `text` so any provider's id format fits).

To flip to Brevo:

1. **Install** `@getbrevo/brevo` (or whatever the current SDK is at flip-time; verify via `npm view @getbrevo/brevo version`).
2. **Add** an env-driven switch: `EMAIL_PROVIDER=resend|brevo` (default `resend`; `brevo` activates the Brevo branch).
3. **Refactor** `src/lib/email.ts` and `src/inngest/functions/send-email.ts` to dispatch on `process.env.EMAIL_PROVIDER`. Each function keeps its signature; the body branches.
4. **Rename** the column or add a sibling column: either rename `email_log.resend_message_id` -> `email_log.provider_message_id` via a Drizzle migration, or add `email_log.brevo_message_id` and write whichever one matches the active provider. Recommended: rename, since `email_log` is audit-only and a one-off rename is cheaper than carrying two columns.
5. **DNS records** — Brevo issues different SPF / DKIM CNAMEs than Resend. The flip plan must (a) add Brevo's records to `command.weknowgroup.com` ALONGSIDE Resend's during the cutover (both providers verified), (b) flip `EMAIL_PROVIDER` env var, (c) wait for in-flight Resend sends to drain, (d) remove Resend's DKIM CNAME after a 7-day quiet period.
6. **Rollback** — flip `EMAIL_PROVIDER=resend` and the Resend records (still in DNS during the cutover window) take effect immediately. If Resend records were already removed, expect a 24-48h DNS-cache lag before deliverability is restored — this is why step 5(d) waits 7 days before pruning.

## What flipping does NOT require

- **No change to Better Auth wiring.** `src/lib/auth.ts:13-24` calls into the three locked email functions; their bodies branch internally on `EMAIL_PROVIDER` so the auth handler is untouched.
- **No change to react-email templates.** `@react-email/render` produces HTML that any HTTP email API accepts.
- **No change to Inngest substrate.** `email/send.requested` event shape is provider-agnostic.
- **No change to `email_log` schema beyond the message-id column** (per step 4).

- **Resend free tier:** 3,000 sends / month at v1.0 close; 100/day. Our v1.1 internal volume (~30 users) tops out around ~900/month worst case.
- **Brevo free tier (at v1.0 close, 2026-04-29):** 300 sends / day (~9,000/month) — strictly more than Resend free. Comfortably covers a fallback period.
- **Both providers** rate-limit per second; expect ~10/sec for transactional sends. Phase 9 digests batch send so no per-second concern.
