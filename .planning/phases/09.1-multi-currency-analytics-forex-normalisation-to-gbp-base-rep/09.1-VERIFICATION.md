### Gaps Summary

The phase delivers the structural FX-normalisation surface correctly: schema, cron, ETL stamping, backfill, analytics dual-emit, renderer dispatch, classifier + commission swap, admin banner, and POC-email contract preservation are all in place and the unit/integration suite passes 83/0. Forex semantics (BoE foreign-per-GBP, GBP identity, carry-forward, 7-day staleness ceiling) are correctly implemented at the data layer.

Four BLOCKER-class gaps remain:

**Gap 1 (newly discovered during verification):** Both `fx_rate_fetch_failed` and `fx_rate_stale` events fire with `template: "plain-text"`, but the send-email Inngest handler's TEMPLATES dispatch has no `plain-text` entry. Any FX alert event throws `Unknown email template: plain-text` before Resend is invoked. The D-08 alert contract is broken at runtime — the cron and ETL signal failure correctly, but the operator never receives an email. This was not flagged by 09.1-REVIEW.md.

**Gap 2 (CR-01):** SQL-injection vectors in regions.ts / location-groups.ts / hotel-groups.ts. Phase 9.1 inherited these patterns when adding GBP columns and did not fix them.
